# Material assets

These locally bundled scans are from Poly Haven, licensed CC0.

- `walnut-*.jpg`: [American Walnut Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/american_walnut_veneer), 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness.
- `studio.hdr`: [Studio Small 09](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09), 1K HDR environment.

[Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license)

These CC0 assets were reused from the sibling Can’t Stop project, which reused
them from Cribbage. Their original provenance and license are retained. The
shelf lab uses the walnut diffuse, normal and roughness scans and the HDR
environment. Plaster, belt ribs, printing and box backs are procedural.
